# S1: The thumb arms only when it lands fully inside the anchor corner

Worktree: `/Users/dominykasgrubys/code/trackpad-launcher/.claude/worktrees/018-thumb-edge-guard` (branch `feature/018-thumb-edge-guard`). Work only there. Do not commit; the orchestrator commits.

## Goal

A contact that lands in the anchor corner within 3 mm of the top edge or the thumb-side edge is an edge contact: it never anchors, never swaps, counts for nothing while it stays down and stays ignored if it rolls inward, in both hand modes and on every trackpad size. A thumb that landed farther in anchors as today and keeps its anchoring if it drifts to the edge. Built test-first (use the `dev-workflow:tdd` skill and `macos-dev-workflow:swift-testing-pro`).

## Requirements

Terms:
- The *anchor corner* covers the top 25% of the pad's height and 20% of its width, measured from the thumb-side edge: top-left in right-hand mode, top-right in left-hand mode.
- The *thumb-side edge* is the pad edge the anchor corner touches at its side: the left edge in right-hand mode, the right edge in left-hand mode.
- The *corner strip* is the part of the anchor corner closer than 3 mm to the top edge or to the thumb-side edge.
- The *tap window* is 400 ms.

- **R1.** A contact whose landing point is in the corner strip MUST NOT become the anchored thumb, so no tap, swipe or window swipe can follow it.
- **R2.** A contact whose landing point is in the corner strip MUST NOT be a thumb tap, so it MUST NOT fire a swap.
- **R3.** While a contact that landed in the corner strip stays down, it MUST NOT add to a tap's finger count. It MUST NOT make the app block other fingers' clicks, scrolling or the system's trackpad swipes. It is not part of a thumb touch.
- **R4.** A contact MUST be judged by where it lands. A contact that landed in the corner strip MUST stay an edge contact when it moves out of the strip. A finger that lifts and lands again MUST be judged by its new landing point.
- **R5.** A thumb that anchored by landing outside the corner strip MUST stay anchored when it moves into the corner strip.
- **R6.** The corner strip MUST cover only contacts landing closer than 3 mm to the top edge or the thumb-side edge. A contact landing exactly 3 mm from both is outside it.
- **R7.** In left-hand mode the corner strip MUST run along the top edge and the right edge of the top-right anchor corner.
- **R8.** The corner strip MUST be 3 mm wide on every trackpad size. This is a physical distance, like the 3 mm band along the bottom edge where a resting palm is ignored.

## Design

All production changes are in two files: `LauncherKit/Sources/LauncherCore/Surface.swift` and `LauncherKit/Sources/LauncherCore/GestureRules.swift`. Nothing else under `LauncherKit/Sources` changes (`git diff --stat main -- LauncherKit/Sources` must list only those two).

- **The corner strip is a third edge band in `EdgeContacts`** (in `Surface.swift`). That filter already judges every contact by its landing point alone, removes edge contacts from the frame before the recognizer sees them, keeps each one out for as long as it stays down, and re-judges a contact that lifts and lands again (ID reuse included). Making the strip one more band in `EdgeContacts.isInBand` delivers R1-R4, R6-R8: a strip contact never reaches `GestureRecognizer`, so it cannot anchor, swap, count, block, or extend a thumb touch.
  - Rejected alternative (do not do it): putting the strip into `AnchorCorner.admits` so a strip landing is a finger. That finger would then count toward a tap under an anchored thumb, open click blocking, extend a thumb touch and stop a later thumb arming, which R3 forbids.
- **`GestureRecognizer` does not change.** Nor do `AnchorCorner.admits`, `AnchorCorner.holds`, `Recognition`, `EdgeContacts`' interface (`init(_ handMode:, surface:)`, `mutating func fingers(in:) -> TouchFrame`), adapters, `DeviceSession`, in-memory trackpads or the model. R5 follows: an anchor is held by `holds`, which knows no strip, and the filter judges landings only.
- **The strip's geometry lives in `AnchorCorner`**, the one definition of the corner. Add one `package` query to `AnchorCorner`: whether a landing point is in the corner strip — true when `admits(p)` is true (the corner's two fractions, strict `<`) AND its distance in millimetres to the top edge (`p.y * surface.heightMM`) or to the thumb-side edge (`handMode.fractionFromThumbSideEdge(x: p.x) * surface.widthMM`) is strictly less than `GestureRules.edgeBandMM`. Name it in the domain language ("corner strip"), consistent in style with `admits`/`holds`, e.g. `stripContains(_:)` or `isInStrip(_:)`. `EdgeContacts` builds an `AnchorCorner` from the same hand mode and surface (store it, or construct it in `init`) and asks it beside its two palm bands in `isInBand`.
  - Rejected: `EdgeContacts` deriving the corner bounds itself from the rules (two definitions of the corner).
- **One band width for every edge.** Reuse `GestureRules.edgeBandMM` (3.0); update its doc comment to name the corner strip as the third place the band applies (an edge contact is "a contact landing closer than 3 mm to a guarded edge"; guarded edges: the whole bottom edge; the hand-side edge below the `edgeBandTop` line; the top edge and the thumb-side edge inside the anchor corner). No new constant, no setting.
- **Left-hand mode** mirrors through `HandMode.fractionFromThumbSideEdge`, which the corner already uses; the top edge is the same in both modes.
- **Doc comments**: update `EdgeContacts`' doc comment (it currently says the band is the bottom edge and the hand-side edge only, "where the heel of the hand rests"; now also the corner strip, where a thumb sits partly off the pad), `AnchorCorner`'s (and its new query's), and `GestureRules.edgeBandMM`'s, to describe the strip.
- Floating point for R6 / T13: the top-edge distance for (3, 3) mm on the 14" surface comes out as 3.0 exactly, and the thumb-side distance via `fractionFromThumbSideEdge` (`1 - (1 - x)`) as 3.000000000000003; both `>= 3`, so the strict `<` reads them outside. Use the strict `<` and the existing helpers; do not add epsilons.
