# S2: Tap-bound settings need Accessibility, not silence

Worktree: `/Users/dominykasgrubys/code/trackpad-launcher/.claude/worktrees/027-tap-to-click-gestures` (branch `feature/027-tap-to-click-gestures`). Use absolute paths. Do not commit; the orchestrator commits your work.

## Goal

With the Accessibility permission, *Tap to click* and *Look up: Tap with three fingers* no longer turn gestures off: gestures, swipes, window swipes and swaps run, and turning a setting on or off while running keeps them running. Without the permission they still turn gestures off, and the settings window says so with an exact sentence and an *Open Accessibility Settings…* button, hiding the Accessibility hint while it does. The old trackpad-settings notice and its *Open Trackpad Settings…* button are gone (also from the no-trackpad notice: the user accepted that); "No trackpad connected" keeps its text and its priority.

This slice does NOT block any tap click, Look up or Smart zoom yet (later slices). It only carries a new `blockTapClicks` flag down to the `TapFilters` both adapters build; nothing reads it yet.

## Requirements

- **R1.** Gestures MUST be active while *Tap to click* or *Look up: Tap with three fingers* is on for any connected trackpad, as long as Trackpad Launcher has the Accessibility permission and a trackpad is connected. This holds for the built-in trackpad and for a Magic Trackpad alike. With the Accessibility permission, no system trackpad setting makes gestures inactive. Scenarios: Tap to click on, a gesture opens its app (with haptic); both settings on for the Magic Trackpad only, the gesture fires on either trackpad; swipe, window swipe and swap behave exactly as with Tap to click off; turning Tap to click on while running keeps gestures active with no relaunch, and the next gesture fires.
- **R6 (in part: activity, what runs, the notice).** While Tap to click or Look up: Tap with three fingers is on for any connected trackpad and Trackpad Launcher lacks the Accessibility permission, gestures MUST be inactive (all five gestures, swipe, window swipe, swap: nothing recognised, nothing fires). With both settings off, gestures without the permission MUST work as they do today. Granting the permission MUST make gestures active within 5 seconds, with no relaunch. Revoking it MUST make them inactive within 5 seconds.
- **R7.** The settings window MUST NOT show the trackpad-settings notice ("Gestures are off while these trackpad settings are on: …") nor its *Open Trackpad Settings…* button, ever.
- **R8.** While gestures are inactive because R6 applies, the settings window MUST show one of these notices, exactly as written:
  - "Gestures are off while Tap to click is on, until Trackpad Launcher has Accessibility access."
  - "Gestures are off while Look up: Tap with three fingers is on, until Trackpad Launcher has Accessibility access."
  - "Gestures are off while Tap to click and Look up: Tap with three fingers are on, until Trackpad Launcher has Accessibility access."
  The notice names only the settings that are on for any connected trackpad. It does not name the trackpad.
- **R9.** The Accessibility hint is the message "Allow Accessibility access to make trackpad gestures work reliably." with its *Open Accessibility Settings…* button, shown while the permission is missing. The R8 notice MUST carry the *Open Accessibility Settings…* button, which opens the Accessibility pane of System Settings. While the notice shows, the Accessibility hint does not. With both settings off and no permission, the Accessibility hint MUST show as it does today.
- **R10.** The R8 notice MUST appear and disappear while the settings window is open, with no need to reopen it.
- **R11.** "No trackpad connected. Connect a trackpad to use gestures." MUST stay as it is today and take priority over every other notice.

## Design

All paths below are under `LauncherKit/`.

1. **Rename** (`Sources/LauncherCore/Activity.swift`): `ConflictingSetting` → `TapBoundSetting`, `ConflictingSettings` → `TapBoundSettings` (the same non-empty, table-ordered value with the failable init). The table itself (Tap to click = key `Clicking`, raw 1 is on; Look up = key `TrackpadThreeFingerTapGesture`, raw 2 is on) and the preferences adapter's reads (`Sources/LauncherPlatform/SystemTrackpadPreferences.swift`) are unchanged. Rename `conflicts(rawValue:)` to a name that says "is on" (e.g. `isOn(rawValue:)`), and rewrite doc comments: a tap-bound setting binds a system action to a tap; gestures coexist with it only through click blocking, which needs Accessibility. Rename everywhere: sources, tests, comments. `grep -rn "Conflicting" LauncherKit docs` must return nothing (from the worktree root).

2. **`InactiveCause`**: `.settings(ConflictingSettings)` becomes `.settingsNeedAccessibility(TapBoundSettings)`. `.noTrackpad` unchanged.

3. **`GestureActivity`** is built from three inputs: the connected kinds, the preference values and the Accessibility grant (`init(connected:values:accessGranted:)` or similar). No trackpad → `.inactive(.noTrackpad)`; otherwise a tap-bound setting on for a connected kind while the grant is missing → `.inactive(.settingsNeedAccessibility(TapBoundSettings))`; otherwise `.active`. One pure helper answers "which tap-bound settings are on for a connected trackpad", in table order (e.g. `TapBoundSetting.on(connected:values:) -> [TapBoundSetting]`, a static on the enum, in core), and feeds both the activity and the run.

4. **`Launcher`** (`Sources/LauncherCore/Launcher.swift`):
   - `activity` now reads the stored grant (`isAccessibilityGranted`). Delete the comment on `isAccessibilityGranted` that says it is kept out of activity "so the icon never reads it" (the menu bar icon no longer exists). Also drop "the icon" from `activity`'s and `GestureActivity`'s doc comments.
   - The reconcile's private `Run` gains `blockTapClicks: Bool`, computed as `blockClicks && Tap to click on for any connected trackpad` (from the helper), so it never holds without `blockClicks` and a Tap to click change while nothing runs changes no `Run`. `blockClicks` stays `active && granted`. A Tap to click change while active and granted therefore re-runs the trackpads (a `Run` change), as a hand-mode or swipe change does.
   - New derived value `inactiveNotice: InactiveNotice?`: nil while gestures are active; otherwise a small core value type (new, in core, e.g. in `Activity.swift` or its own file) holding `message: String` (from `Copy.inactiveNotice(for:)`) and `button: NoticeButton?` where `NoticeButton` is an enum with one case `.openAccessibilitySettings`. `.settingsNeedAccessibility` → button `.openAccessibilitySettings`; `.noTrackpad` → no button. Equatable, Sendable.
   - New derived value `showsAccessibilityHint: Bool` = grant missing AND `inactiveNotice` carries no button. (So with no trackpad and no grant the hint still shows beside the no-trackpad notice, as today.)

5. **`TrackpadHardware.run`** (`Sources/LauncherCore/Ports.swift`) gains one input `blockTapClicks: Bool`. Doc contract to add: presses that arrive while a lift window is open are blocked; only meaningful together with `blockClicks`. Both adapters (`Sources/LauncherPlatform/MultitouchTrackpads.swift`, which installs `ClickTap`; and the fake `InMemoryTrackpads` in `Tests/LauncherCoreTests/InMemoryAdapters.swift`) hand it to the `TapFilters` they build, where it is fixed for that tap's life: `TapFilters(blocksTapClicks:)` stored as a `let` on `TapFilters` (`Sources/LauncherCore/TapFilters.swift`); nothing per event carries it. `ClickTap` builds its filters at `ClickTap.swift:15` (`Mutex(TapFilters())`): give `ClickTap.install` the flag. Existing `TapFilters()` call sites in tests can use a default argument `blocksTapClicks: false` if you add one, or pass it explicitly; your choice, keep it simple. Nothing reads the flag yet in this slice.

6. **`Copy`** (`Sources/LauncherCore/Copy.swift`): `inactiveNotice(for:)` gives, for `.settingsNeedAccessibility`, "Gestures are off while " + names joined in English with "and" (today's `.formatted(.list(type: .and).locale(Locale(identifier: "en")))` gives "A and B" for two) + (" is on" for one, " are on" for two) + ", until Trackpad Launcher has Accessibility access."; for `.noTrackpad` the unchanged sentence. Delete `Copy.trackpadSettingsButton`.

7. **Settings window** (`Sources/LauncherUI/`):

   - `SettingsView.swift`: render the notice from `launcher.inactiveNotice`: its message, and the *Open Accessibility Settings…* button (`Copy.accessibilityButton`, opening `actions.openSettings(.accessibility)`, the same open-pane action the hint uses) only when the value carries `.openAccessibilitySettings`. `SettingsNotice` takes an optional button (message alone when none). ## Rules that apply

- architecture.md, Shape: "`LauncherCore` (domain types, pure policy, ports, the models; imports Foundation and Observation only)". The new notice value and helper live in core with no AppKit/SwiftUI.
- architecture.md, Shape: "Policy lives in pure functions and value types; the shell stays thin." The view does not decide which notice or button shows; the model does.
- architecture.md, Shape: "Every port has exactly two adapters: the real one in `LauncherPlatform` and an in-memory one in the tests." Change `TrackpadHardware.run` in both.
- architecture.md, Shape: "Framework and wire types never cross a port … `UserDefaults` keys … stay inside adapters."
- architecture.md, Concurrency: "No timers, no polling, no background loops: every input is a push (… cfprefs KVO, … the TCC Darwin notification …)." R10 must ride the existing pushes.
- domain-model.md, *Tap-bound setting*: "A system trackpad setting that binds a system action to a tap rather than a press. Today: *Tap to click* and *Look up: Tap with three fingers*. The list is one table in the code; a setting is *on* when it is on for any connected trackpad. Gestures coexist with a tap-bound setting only through click blocking, which needs the Accessibility permission: while one is on and the permission is missing, gestures are inactive."
- domain-model.md, *Gestures active / inactive*: "Gestures are *active* when Trackpad Launcher is running, at least one trackpad is connected, and either no tap-bound setting is on or Trackpad Launcher has the Accessibility permission. Otherwise … inactive … Granting or revoking the permission, and turning a tap-bound setting on or off, change this live, with no relaunch."
- domain-model.md, *No trackpad notice*: "… shown while no trackpad is connected, with no button. It takes priority over the tap-bound settings notice."
- domain-model.md, *Tap-bound settings notice*: "… Its *Open Accessibility Settings…* button opens the Accessibility pane of System Settings. While it shows, the Accessibility hint does not. … The settings window never asks the user to turn a trackpad setting off."
- domain-model.md, *Accessibility hint*: "shown while Trackpad Launcher lacks the Accessibility permission and the tap-bound settings notice does not show".
- Use the glossary words in names and comments: *tap-bound setting*, *gestures active/inactive*, *no trackpad notice*, *tap-bound settings notice*, *Accessibility hint*. Never "conflicting".
