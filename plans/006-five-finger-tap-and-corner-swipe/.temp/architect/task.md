## What the design must address

Every requirement R1–R24 must be carried by a named type, signature or decision, with each scenario traceable through the usage and the sketch. The grounding index lists the facts (F1–F35) and the open design decisions (O1–O10); a design settles each O with a decision and cites the F it rests on. In particular:

1. **Recognition on the frame path.** The fifth finger count (R1). The swipe as part of `GestureRecognizer` or beside it on the same frame thread: begins on the first sideways movement past 3 mm of either of exactly two non-thumb fingers that landed under an anchored thumb, with no time limit (R3), while a tap keeps its 400 ms and its 3 mm drag rule; a vertical first movement spoils until every non-thumb finger lifts (R3); the swipe survives the thumb lifting, leaving the corner or re-landing (R8, R9); ends on the first finger lift (R8); cancels on a third finger outside the corner or a delivered click, never on a blocked press (R9); "nothing further until every non-thumb finger has lifted" after end or cancel; `blocksClicks` (or its successor) stays true for the whole swipe regardless of the thumb and the 3 s window (R13); travel is the sideways midpoint displacement in trackpad millimetres, positive to the right in both hand modes (R6). No timer, no second clock (`docs/architecture.md`, F8). State the per-frame output type and what crosses the hardware port to the main actor (events, their payload, their ordering guarantee: F6, F7).
2. **Selection and the switcher.** Travel → selection and highlight position as pure, testable policy: icons 12 mm apart, selection = nearest icon, so it changes at 6 mm then every 12 mm, reversible, hard stops with a small overshoot that springs back (R6); the row = switcher list in order minus missing apps, with the frontmost app prepended when absent (R4); selection starts on the frontmost (R5); no switcher when the row would be empty or only the frontmost (R11); a light tick per selection change (R7); on end: bring to front, gesture haptic, no launch animation, window closes, silent no-op when the selection is the frontmost (R8); on cancel: nothing (R9). Decide where the frontmost app is read and parsed into an `AppEntry` (F1–F3), what happens when it is nil or Trackpad Launcher itself (O6), and how the icons are ready in time (F4, F5).
3. **The switcher on screen (R10).** A Liquid Glass panel centred on the pointer's display, 80 pt icons 20 pt apart, a glass highlight that follows the fingers, the selected icon slightly larger with its name below, appearing with no perceptible delay, gone within 200 ms, never key, never activating, above full-screen apps on every Space, light and dark, Reduce motion honoured. Decide whether it is model state rendered by `LauncherUI` or an effect behind a port played by `LauncherPlatform` (O5), citing the architecture's shell/adapter split and the launch overlay's panel configuration (F26, F27, F32).
4. **Scroll suppression (R12–R14).** Through the existing event tap: trackpad and Magic Mouse scrolling suppressed under exactly the click-blocking condition and for the whole swipe, a wheel mouse never (F18: `isContinuous`), pointer movement never; the momentum after a suppressed scroll suppressed, the momentum after an unsuppressed one untouched (R14), remembering that momentum arrives after the fingers lifted; without the permission nothing is suppressed (F20). Decide drop versus zeroed deltas (O4) and the exact memory rule. Update the source-policy gate structurally (F17: banned → confined, the overlap self-check, `PointerEventTests`), never by weakening it; keep `CGEvent` types inside `LauncherPlatform`.
5. **The launcher window (R2, R15–R22, R24).** A fifth row and a switcher section (illustration + "Swipe to switch apps", rows with icon and name, "not found" greyed, hover remove button and context-menu Remove, drag reorder, "Add app…" as a picker-shaped menu with listed apps checked and disabled and no None, Other… appending without duplicates, empty state), the hand-mode toggle below it, the amended hint copies. The window grows with the list and, when it would not fit the visible frame, the list scrolls inside while everything else stays reachable (R22; F28). Reuse the picker machinery and the icon cache (F29); say how the menu shape stays a pure core value.
6. **Persistence (R23).** The fifth assignment and the ordered switcher list in `Settings`, with a decoder that loads a record saved by today's build (F22) and the first-launch rule intact (F23).
7. **Tests.** Name the seam each requirement's scenarios run at and the handle a test needs for everything it cannot wait for or provoke: the frontmost app, the switcher's visible state, the haptic kind, the swipe event stream, scroll events (synthetic `CGEvent`s), the old settings record, and the `TouchScript` primitives a swipe script needs (F12). The in-memory hardware must keep delivering synchronously (F7).
8. **Docs.** Say which `docs/domain-model.md` entries change (the plan's Solution lists them) and whether `docs/architecture.md` needs a core-decision change (only if one of its cross-cutting rules changes: e.g. what the Accessibility permission is used for).

## What the human asked for that bears on the design

The original brief, verbatim: "I want to do few new gestures and settings for them: 5 fingers tap, works same as other gestures, basically we just add additional identical gesture and setting; 2 fingers swipe while holding thumb on a corner, which allows human to navigate through more apps. In settings user can select any amount of apps, usually it would be apps, that didn't fit the 5 gestures. When user hold a thumb on left top corner, and start swiping with 2 fingers left or right we show the row of app icons, initially selecting the middle one and user can swipe to whichever he wants to open, when user releases fingers the app icon which is selected is being opened. Oh, actually initially we always show the currently opened app icon, so user can stay with the same app. The indication of the swipe should be very clear, both when you are on the selected app icon, and when you are nearing the next icon. Basically, we should indicate the currently selected icon during the swipe, but also kinda indicate the swipe progress, so you can feel how much you need to swipe to other icons, and in general feel that swiping responsive. There is a setting inside menu bar window, where user see the list of apps applied to the swipe gesture and there is an option to reorder them, remove them and add new ones. You should try to make design consistent and use latest human design interface patterns. In the swipe selector, icon size should be 80pt."

Decisions the human made later, already in the requirements: the thumb only starts a swipe and plays no part afterwards; scrolling is suppressed under the click-blocking rule and for the whole of a swipe; no launch animation on a swipe release; a row wider than the screen is not handled.

Facts the human established on the hardware: a thumb anchored plus two fingers sliding produces ordinary two-finger scrolling and nothing else (no Spaces or page swipe). `assets/` has no 5-finger or swipe illustration; both are drawn during implementation in the style of the existing SVGs (48×31, `#232323`).

## Constraints that are not negotiable

`docs/architecture.md` in full: Swift 6 strict concurrency; `LauncherCore` imports Foundation and Observation only; every port has exactly two adapters; framework and wire types never cross a port; no timers, polling or display links; one writer per field; recognition on the frame thread, only completed events hop to main; the shell stays thin and renders model state; `SourcePolicyTests` is the mechanism for every "never" (add to its lists, never prose). The existing tests' behaviours that this plan does not amend must keep passing; the ones it amends are named in the grounding (`how.md` "Where the planned changes land").
