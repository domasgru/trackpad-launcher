# S1: The guarded band follows the hand mode

**What to build:** From the user's side: with the thumb anchored, a tap near the top edge, near the edge opposite the hand side, or near the upper part of the hand-side edge now fires its gesture, counts in a two-finger tap, takes part in a swipe and a swap, and blocks clicks and the system's swipes like any finger in the middle of the pad; a palm's side brushing the bottom edge or the lower part of the hand-side edge is still ignored, in both hand modes; a finger that lands just above the line and slides into the band stays a finger. Underneath: the band constant for the 40% line beside the 3 mm one; `EdgeContacts` built from the hand mode and the surface, deciding the band from the bottom edge and the hand-side edge below the line, with the anchor-corner carve-out gone; the recognizer passing its hand mode; the nearest-edge helper removed from `SurfaceSize`; the edge-contact suite's band test extended into T1 and its boundary test renamed; the glossary already updated in this plan.
