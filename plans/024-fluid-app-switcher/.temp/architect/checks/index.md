# Checks gathered after fan-out

Evidence gathered after the runners started, so it sits outside the frozen grounding. All checks ran on the same machine as the grounding probes (macOS 26.6.2), on 2026-11-03.

- **K1. probed (C1, output in `c1-output.txt`).** A panel created with `defer: true` has no layer, and so no glass mirror, until it is first ordered front. Right after `orderFrontRegardless()` it still has none. After `CATransaction.flush()` the `CASDFElementLayer`s exist, one per glass view.
- **K2. probed (C1).** A glass view parked far outside the window is still mirrored. It grows the `CASDFLayer` and backdrop bounds to the union of element frames: 4040 pt wide for a view parked at x = 4000. Conclusion: parking spares outside the window enlarges the render area.
- **K3. probed (C2, output in `c2-output.txt`).** With a fixed set of 12 glass views matched once by frame while every frame was distinct, the matched layers stayed live and at their views' frames (12 of 12) across order-out, re-layout for a 4-app row by frame sets only, and order-in. Spares laid exactly over the last real bead kept the SDF bounds to the real row (388 pt for 4 apps).
- **K4. probed (C2).** `isHidden = true` on one glass view drops its element within 0.3 s. Unhiding creates a new element object, so the matched layer is no longer live (11 of 12). Conclusion: hiding or removing glass views forces a re-match.
- **K5. probed (C1).** Changing membership (removing a view) is mirrored lazily: it was not reflected after two run loop turns. After the next order-out and order-in, every element was a new object.
- **K6. probed (C4, output in `c4-output.txt`).** An in-process A/B: six spares laid exactly over the last bead left the settled glass brightness identical (0.800 with and without). The outline moved by 2 pt at most, and that drift stayed after the spares were removed, so the spares did not cause it. Conclusion: coincident spares are visually neutral.
- **K7. probed (C4).** The glass settles over its first seconds on screen. Inside brightness went from 0.914 at 1 s to 0.800 a few seconds later over a mid-grey backdrop. This is system behaviour of the material, not ours.
- **K8. probed (C3, captures `c3-*.png`).** The C3 runs were separate processes and differ in brightness because of K7. Compare outlines only. Coincident spares keep the outline (`c3-coincident.png` against `c3-none.png`). Note: the brightness threshold C3 printed failed on the darkest capture (`none`), so its printed numbers are not usable.
- **K9. probed (C5, output in `c5-output.txt`).** AppKit mirrors a glass view frame set into its element during our transaction's commit, not at the setter. Inside the transaction the element still read 38; right after `commit()` it read 138. Conclusion: a view frame set and an additive track added in the same transaction reach the render server together. Offsets must be computed from the view's new rest, never read back from the element inside the transaction. A one-frame jump was not observed frame by frame.
- **K10. probed (C5).** Sampled keyframe tracks per event were measured over 200 events at 100 Hz: 3 additive `CAKeyframeAnimation`s of 240 samples on the swell element every event, plus 10 bead tracks of 150 samples every 5th event, with the samples computed each time. Process CPU was **139.9 ms over 2 s (about 7% of a core)**, with about one extra main wake-up per event. Compare A7: held additive basic animations about 2.5%, public frame sets about 26%, today's switcher about 11%.
- **K12. probed (C6, output in `c6-output.txt`), Phase C.** A deferred panel was ordered front once with its content layer at opacity 0, or with the window's `alphaValue` at 0, then flushed: the mirror was built and all 12 elements matched uniquely by frame. After ordering out, re-laying out for a 4-app row (spares exactly over the band) and ordering in at opacity 1, the match stayed live and placed (12 of 12), both right after the flush and 0.5 s later. The region captured during the pre-warm showed nothing of ours, and the first swipe showed the 4-app shape with no trace of the spares. Those captures were viewed and then deleted, because the region also held another app's window.
- **K11. probed (C5).** Finished, filled-forward additive animations cost nothing at rest. WindowServer CPU over 5 s with 11 to 13 held animations was 0.48 and 0.49 s, against 0.47 and 0.49 s with none, and 2.28 s with one running animation. Our main wake-ups were the same either way (27 to 28 in 5 s, the check's own `ps` spawns).
