# Testing: Taps near the top edge count

## Testing Decisions

### Strategy

One seam, existing: `GestureRecognizer` over scripted frames in `LauncherKit/Tests/LauncherCoreTests/`, the pure recognizer stepped frame by frame, observing per frame what fired, every swipe phase with its travel, the swap event, and both blocking flags. The frames come from `TouchScript`, `HandFrames` and the edge suite's `Track` helper; `fired`, `phases` and `swaps` from `Fixtures.swift` and the suite's own `firedAt`, `swipeEvents` and `recognitions` read the results. The new rows resemble `EdgeContactRecognizerTests.swift` throughout (its parametrised band rows, its resting-palm row, its sliding-finger row and its third-contact swipe row), `SwapRecognizerTests.swift` for the swap event and `ClickBlockingRecognizerTests.swift` for the per-frame flags, and they live in the edge suite, which is the suite for this rule; T1 is that suite's existing band test extended rather than a new one beside it.

What the seam misses: the model's reaction to what the recognizer reports, a switcher on `.began`, an app brought to front on a swap, a press dropped on `blocksClicks`. No line of that changes here, and `SwitcherOnScreenTests`, `SwapTests` and `ClickBlockingTests` prove it through `World` for fingers in the middle of the pad; since T4–T8 prove the recognizer reports a freed finger's tap, swipe, swap and flags identically to a middle finger's, the model cannot tell the two apart. The `World` seam would run the same recognizer over the same frames and add only that unchanged path, while hiding the per-frame flags behind press verdicts that need access granted. The seam also misses the real hand. This is a requirement change the human confirmed, not a bug, so no reproduction of the top-edge taps is recorded. The slice's hardware check is plan 007's palm recording replayed after the change (nothing fires), with its palm landings, all 11 mm or more below the 40% line, as the evidence that the palm's side stays in the band; a by-hand check on the trackpad is optional, for the human after the PR.

Faked: nothing. The recognizer is a pure value and the clock is the frame timestamps.

Default fixture, unless a row says otherwise: `macBook14` (124.8 × 76.8 mm); thumb at (10, 10) in right-hand mode and at (114.8, 10) in left-hand mode; the middle finger at (62, 45); 10 ms frames; swipes and swaps on; `.linear` travel. Positions are millimetres from the top-left corner. "n% down" is n% of the height: on `macBook14` 20% = 15.36, 30% = 23.04, 35% = 26.88, 38% = 29.18, 45% = 34.56, 70% = 53.76; on `magicTrackpad` (160 × 115) 35% = 40.25, 45% = 51.75. "1 mm from the right edge" is x = 123.8 (x = 159 on `magicTrackpad`); from the left edge x = 1; from the top edge y = 1; from the bottom edge y = 75.8. The anchor corner's right side in right-hand mode is x = 24.96 and its bottom y = 19.2, so "5 mm right of the corner" is x = 30 and "just below the corner" on the left edge is 30% down. The middle of the width is x = 62.4. No row sits on the 40% line itself: in normalised coordinates an exact 40.000% is meaningless, so the rows sit at 35%, 38% and 45%, as the 3 mm rows sit at 2.5 and 3.5 mm.

Before and after: T2, T4, T5, T6, T8 and T7's top-edge row fail on today's code, where every edge is guarded. T1, T3 and T7's middle-finger row pass today: they are the guards that keep the band where the palm rests while the rest is freed, and each names the wrong band it catches.

### Verified without a test

- **R6, contacts away from the edges behave as today.** The whole existing suite stays green through `Scripts/test.sh`, and `git diff main -- LauncherKit/Tests` edits no `@Test` outside `EdgeContactRecognizerTests.swift`; inside it, the band test becomes T1 (its three freed rows deleted, superseded by T2's; its guarded rows replaced by T1's), the 3 mm boundary test is renamed, and nothing else changes, as the design's "Existing tests" prescribes. Every other contact in the suite lands 10 mm or more from the top, left and right edges (the nearest is the left-hand thumb at x = 114.8), or inside the anchor corner (the corner-thumb guard at (1, 1) and (123.8, 1), never in the band), or inside the band that stays (the right edge between 52% and 78% down and the bottom edge, the palm in `SwapTests.swift` included), so a green run proves the change touched nothing for them. A new test cannot prove "as today" better than the tests that defined today's behaviour, and the scenario's middle-of-the-pad tap is `GestureRecognizerTests.thumbThenNFingerTapFiresThatGestureOnceAndSixFireNothing` already.
