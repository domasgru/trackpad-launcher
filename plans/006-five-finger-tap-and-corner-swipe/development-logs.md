- User report after merge review: swiping in the switcher sometimes also moved to another desktop. Not reproduced (intermittent); diagnosed from the code and settings. This Mac has the three-finger horizontal swipe set to switch desktops, and macOS can count a thumb that landed recently or shifts as a finger, so thumb plus two sliding fingers reads as that three-finger swipe. The tap never saw it: its mask held only clicks, pressure and scroll, and the Dock's swipe travels as a separate gesture event. Fix: the tap also takes event types 29 (gesture) and 30 (Dock control) and drops only Dock swipes (HID type 23, field 110; phase in field 132), via a pure `DockSwipeFilter` that judges each Dock swipe once on its opening event and keeps it whole, since dropping one mid-way could strand the Dock between desktops. Its flag, `Recognition.blocksDockSwipes`, has no time limit: a thumb anchored with another contact down, or a swipe on. The field numbers are undocumented and taken from reverse-engineered Dock swipe tools; a hardware check must confirm the tap sees and drops real Dock swipes.
