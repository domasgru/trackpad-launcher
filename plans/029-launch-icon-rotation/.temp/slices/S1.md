# S1: The launch animation leaves the pointer along a slanted line, tuned by Max slant

Worktree: `/Users/dominykasgrubys/code/trackpad-launcher/.claude/worktrees/029-launch-icon-rotation` (branch `feature/029-launch-icon-rotation`). All paths below are relative to it. Do not commit; leave the work in the tree.

## Goal

Every launch animation (and every bounce ending that plays one) rises along a straight line slanted toward its sway's side from the first moment it moves, by an angle of share × Max slant, where share is the sway's drawn size share. The sway's late veer adds on top unchanged; the pop, the rise's height and timing, and the tilt are untouched; Reduce motion still fades in place. The Debug tuner's Launch tab gets a *Max slant* slider (0…30°, 0.5° steps, shipped 4°) that the Launch reset restores, saves carry, and older saves load at 4°.

Glossary: *slant* = angle between the path and straight up, toward the sway's side. *Veer* = the sway's late sideways part (today's whole sway). *Drift* = all sideways movement, veer + slant.

## Requirements

- **R1.** Once its pop ends, the icon MUST move along a line slanted from straight up toward the side its sway veers to, from the first moment it moves. The rise's height and timing stay the same. The slant adds sideways movement equal to height risen × tan(slant angle), so the path from the start of the rise is a straight line at that angle, before the veer adds on top. The side is random, and a side never comes up three times in a row. During the pop the icon stays centred on the pointer.
- **R2.** The slant's share of Max slant MUST equal the sway's size share for that launch (the share between Min sway and 1 the launch drew), in direct proportion — in angle, not in tangent. The slant does not depend on Max sway. Shipped: largest sway 4°, share 0.55 → 2.2°, smallest (share 0.1) → 0.4°. With Max sway 0 and Max slant 4° a launch still slants toward a side picked by the same side rule, with no late veer.
- **R3.** Shipped: Max slant 4°, Max sway 0.26, Min sway 0.1, Sway curve 1.8.
- **R4.** The shipped numbers keep the icon's sideways distance from its start ≤ a third of its rise at every moment (the ratio only grows, so the end is the worst moment).
- **R5.** With Reduce motion on, or the tuner's Simulate Reduce motion on, the icon fades out in place with no slant.
- **R6.** Every bounce ending that plays a launch animation slants like any other, from the icon's first rising moment, whether or not it pops, from the spot the icon is at.
- **R7.** The tuner's Launch tab shows a *Max slant* slider in its Sway section, 0°…30° in 0.5° steps, that changes the maximum slant of the next launch animation.
- **R8.** The Launch tab's reset sets Max slant back to 4°; the other tabs' resets leave it alone.
- **R9.** A tuning saved with Max slant at a value loads with that value.
- **R10.** A tuning saved before this change loads with Max slant 4° and every saved value kept.

### Step 3 — the slant

`LaunchFlight`'s moving motion replaces today's one signed sway value (`case sway(CGFloat)`, from which the tilt back-computes its lean by dividing by Max sway) with a **side** (the existing private `Side` enum, left/right, whose `sign` is −1/+1) and a **share**: `Min sway + (1 − Min sway) × min(|draw|, 1)`, in Min sway…1. Side picking is unchanged (zero → right; a third same side in a row is swapped, keeping the size).

The pose after the pop, at rise share `u` (the post-pop re-normalised u, as today), exactly:

```
rise = Rise × u^riseCurve
x    = sign(side) × (rise × tan(share × Max slant) + share × Max sway × rise × u^swayCurve)
y    = rise
lean = Max sway > 0 ? share : 0
tilt = −sign(side) × lean × Max tilt × u^tiltCurve      (counter-clockwise positive; Max slant and Max tilt are degrees → radians)
```

- The slant angle is `share × maximumSlant` in degrees, converted to radians, then `tan`: the share scales the angle, not its tangent.
- At the shipped numbers the veer and tilt are exactly today's. The tilt's lean keeps today's guard: with Max sway 0 the icon slants but does not tilt.
- The pop is untouched: no offset during the pop. The slant starts with the rise, pop or no pop.
- Size, shrink, fade, the Reduce-motion branch: unchanged.

`LaunchTuning.swift`:
- Add `var maximumSlant: Double = 4` (degrees) after `swayCurve`, documented as: the slant of the largest sway, in degrees; a launch's slant is its sway's share × Max slant; the path leans this way from the first rising moment.
- Doc comments: both `maximumSway`'s and `maximumSlant`'s state the third-of-rise inequality on the shipped numbers: `tan(Max slant) + Max sway ≤ 1/3` at the largest sway (shipped: tan 4° + 0.26 = 0.3299), so the drift never outgrows a third of the rise. `swayCurve`'s comment calls the sway's part the **veer**, not the drift (e.g. "Veer = sway × rise(t) × t^swayCurve. Higher veers later.").
- Debug decoder `init(from:)`: `maximumSlant = try c.decode(.maximumSlant, or: s.maximumSlant)` (missing-or-shipped like every key). Encoding stays synthesized.
- `resettingToShipped(.launch)` also resets `maximumSlant`; the other tabs do not touch it.
