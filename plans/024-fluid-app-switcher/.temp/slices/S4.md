# S4: The swell stretches toward the fingers and pulls past the ends

## Goal

Between selection changes, the swell's leading edge follows the fingers toward the next icon while its trailing edge and the enlarged icon stay pinned, and it springs back when they return. Past the first or last app, only the swell's outer edge pulls outward, up to a fifth of a pitch, for as long as the fingers keep moving. When they stop moving or turn back, it springs back around the end icon with a wobble. This slice is LauncherCore only, in `Liquid`. It uses the same three swell channels, so the UI does not change.

## Requirements

- **R5.** While the fingers slide but the selection has not changed yet, the swell's trailing edge and the enlarged icon MUST NOT move. Only the swell's leading edge extends toward the direction of the slide, and it grows with the distance travelled. When the fingers slide back, the swell MUST spring back toward the selected icon.
  - *Slide just under half a step:* with the first app selected, the fingers slide right 5 mm, short of the 6 mm that changes the selection. The swell still wraps the first icon, its left edge and the enlarged icon stay put, and its right edge visibly reaches toward the second.
  - *Slide back before the change:* the fingers slide back to where they started. The swell springs back around the first icon and settles there.
- **R9 (this slice's part).** When the fingers stop moving, the liquid MUST settle with a small wobble within one second. Once settled, it MUST NOT move again until the fingers move.
- **R10.** The selection stops at the first and last app. Finger travel past an end MUST pull only the swell's outer edge outward past that end icon, like a drop being pulled. The band and the other beads MUST NOT move. The pull grows with the extra travel and MUST NOT exceed a fifth of the distance between two icon centres (20 pt at full size). Most of it is reached within 3 mm past the end. The pull lasts only while the fingers keep moving past the end: when they stop moving or turn back, the swell MUST spring back around the end icon with a wobble.
  - *Push past the last app:* the swell's right edge stretches out past the last icon while the band and other beads stay put. It stops growing at 20 pt, and springs back around the last icon with a wobble when the fingers slide left.
  - *Rest past the last app:* the fingers stop moving, still past the end. The swell springs back around the last icon with a wobble and settles there, though the fingers have not turned back.
- **R12 (this slice's part).** *Lift during a stretch:* with the first app (the app in front) selected and the swell stretched toward the second, the fingers lift. No app changes and the switcher closes.
- **R14 (the pull at scale).** The pull's cap scales with the row: a fifth of the pitch.
- **R15.** The liquid MUST respond to finger movement without visible lag.

## Design

This is all in `LauncherKit/Sources/LauncherCore/Liquid.swift` (with `EdgeCurve`). Nothing in LauncherUI changes.

Today, under `.flowing`, a call whose selection value changed but whose `selected` did not returns `.empty`. This slice replaces that branch. It also folds the lean into the flow's targets. The rules:

- **Every call that is a new value** (`selection != last`) retargets both edges from their closed-form state at `time`. Nothing else changes about how the values arrive: every value is an absolute target, and coalesced or repeated values must give the same settled shape.
- **Targets inside the row.**
  - lean = `selection.position − selected`, in items, signed.
  - The edge on the lean's side (the right edge for lean > 0, the left for lean < 0) targets its rest edge + `reach` × lean × pitch (a negative lean moves the left edge left).
  - The other edge targets its rest edge exactly. With lean 0, both target their rest edges.
  - The rest is `layout.swell(at: selected)`.
- **Selection changed.** The flow from S3, with the targets above: the edge on the side the selection moved toward arrives over `leadShare × T` and the other over T, with T = min(`flowTime`, 0.2). The swell rest and the icon change are staged as in S3.
- **Selection unchanged, inside the row (the follow).**
  - Both edges approach their targets over `followTime` (1/60 s, the glide between trackpad frames).
  - The leading edge arrives with the estimated finger velocity: the change in its target since the last call, divided by the time since then, with an 8 ms floor on that time. The arrival is clamped so its damped tail stays within `wobble` × scale, the same clamp as S3.
  - The pinned edge must not move at all. An edge whose target equals its current value, with zero velocity, stays put exactly: arrival 0, value constant to 1e-6 pt.
  - No swell rest and no icon change are staged. Only the three swell tracks.
- **Held offsets.** When the lean is non-zero and the fingers rest inside the row, the leading edge's curve ends on its target, which is off the rest. That track's last offset is non-zero, so it holds (`LiquidTrack.holds`), and the panel keeps it filled forwards. Finished, filled animations cost nothing at rest.
- **Past an end (the end pull).**
  - "Past an end" means `selection.travelMM` is below 0 or above (count − 1) × 12, with `selected` at that end. The overshoot o, in step-mm, is the distance beyond it. `position` is clamped there, so the lean is 0.
  - The pull is P × (1 − e^(−o / `endPullMM`)), with P = min(`endPull` × scale, `endPullBound` × pitch), where `endPullBound` = 0.2. At o = 3 mm and the shipped look that is 19 pt, "most of it within 3 mm".
  - Only the **outer** edge moves: the right edge past the last app, the left edge past the first. It approaches rest edge ± pull over `followTime`, and **stays there until `pullHold`** (60 ms) after this call. Then it **springs back** to its rest with a wobble: an approach over T = min(`flowTime`, 0.2), arriving with an inward velocity at the wobble clamp bound so the rest is crossed at least twice, then the damped, windowed tail. The curve ends exactly at the rest no later than `settleBound` after this call. So a pull track always ends at 0 and never holds.
  - The **inner** edge stays at its rest exactly.
  - Each new selection value past the end restarts this from the edge's current state, so a continuous push, even one the model clamps, keeps the pull out. `SwitcherSelection`'s synthesised `==` includes the reported travel, so a push against the clamp is still a new value. Resting fingers spring it back after `pullHold`.
  - **The cap.** The outer edge's whole curve, wobble and Hermite overshoot included, must stay within P beyond its rest at every instant. Clamp the outer edge's value at rest ± P wherever it is evaluated (the sampler and `shape(at:)` alike), or choose arrivals that make overshoot impossible. Either way, the sampled tracks and `shape(at:)` stay identical closed forms.
- **Turning back from past an end.** The model forgets the overshoot (travel restarts at the end plus the frame's movement), so the next value is inside the row with a small lean toward the inside. The outer edge then targets its rest, and the inner edge leads. An edge that is coming back from a pull to its rest uses the spring-back leg above (arriving inward at the clamp bound, with a wobble) rather than the plain follow. That is how T25 (b) gets its wobble from the turn.
- **`EdgeCurve`** gains what the pull needs: a hold at the target until a given time, then a second leg back to a rest, the whole windowed to end at that rest at most `settleBound` after `start`. Keep it the single motion primitive. Flow, finger follow, spring-back and end pull are parameter choices of it.
- **Thinning is already built** (S3): the swell's height = rest height − `thinning` × max(0, width − rest width), clamped to [`selectedIconBody` + 4 × scale, rest height]. Check that it holds for the lean and the pull too.
- **Under `.still`,** nothing changes: every call that keeps `selected` returns `.empty`, so nothing stretches past an end (R13).
- **No bead tracks** in this slice. S5 adds the wave, and it emits no crest past an end.

What `follow` documents, at the end of this slice:

> The first call returns the settled opening (R11): the swell's rest, the icon at once, no tracks. Later calls return only what changed. On a selection change, that is the swell's new rest and the growing icon. They also return the tracks that replace their channels' running ones, each starting where and as fast as its channel is at `time`. The frame is empty when `selection` equals the last one fed: a repeated call, and resting fingers. `SwitcherSelection`'s `==` includes the travel last reported, so every move is a new value, even a push against the clamp past an end, which re-pulls the swell (R10). Under `.still`, every call that keeps `selected` is empty.

## Rules that apply

- `docs/architecture.md`, Shape: "`LauncherCore` (domain types, pure policy, ports, the models; imports Foundation and Observation only)". Plain `Double`s only.
- `docs/architecture.md`, Concurrency: "Animation is Core Animation keyframes, which the render server plays. … No code of ours runs per display frame … Code may run per input event while the effect it serves is on screen." Every curve is staged whole at the event and ends by itself: no curve may rely on a later call to finish it.
- `docs/domain-model.md`, Switcher: "While they slide, the swell's leading edge reaches toward the next icon, its trailing edge and the enlarged icon staying put. … The ends are hard stops that add no dead zone: travel past an end pulls only the swell's outer edge outward, at most a fifth of the distance between two icon centres, for as long as the fingers keep moving. When they stop moving, the swell springs back around the end icon with a wobble. The moment the fingers turn back, it springs back too, travel past the end is forgotten, and the next icon is the usual half icon away."
- Requirement bounds are `Liquid`'s private constants (`flowBound`, `settleBound`, `endPullBound`): "no look can break them: flow at most 0.2 s, settle at most 1 s, end pull at most 0.2 pitch."

Notes on reading the rows:

- **"Pull"** is the outer edge's distance beyond the end icon's swell rest edge.
- **T25 "every call returns a non-empty frame" while the push continues:** the model sends a `.moved` value on every frame the fingers move, and each is a new `SwitcherSelection`. The helper's `rest(forMS:)` feeds unchanged values; those must stay empty.
- **T28** is "where it would settle" given only the frames up to that event: build a `LiquidRun` from that prefix of calls and read its settled shape.
- **Every earlier row must stay green: T1 to T22.**
  - S3 rows that ramp travel (T8, T10, T12, T13) now also see lean tracks between changes. Their assertions are about the rests, reach times and settling. Keep them true without loosening them. If one genuinely conflicts with the lean, report it rather than weakening it silently.
  - T17 ("each non-empty frame's swell tracks last until the next non-empty frame, or hold their last offset") now covers lean frames too.

**Checks by hand** (for the human; do not attempt them): the stretch toward the next icon and the end pull read as a drop being pulled. The pull springs back with a wobble when the fingers rest past the end and when they turn back. A slow, steady push past the end stays pulled rather than pulsing (`pullHold`).

## Starting point

S1 to S3 are committed.

- **`Liquid`** (`Sources/LauncherCore/Liquid.swift`) holds the last selection and two `EdgeCurve`s (`left`, `right`). Its parts:
  - `follow`: the opening; empty on a repeat; `flow(from:to:at:)` on a change, with the lead/trail approaches and the clamped arrivals; and `.empty` when the value changed but `selected` did not (this slice replaces that).
  - `height(stretchedBy:rest:)`: the thinning with its clamp, already built.
  - `tracks(over:from:)`: samples the three swell channels at 120 Hz from the call's time to the curves' end, against the given rest.
  - `shape(at:)`: the same closed forms.
- **`EdgeCurve`** has a Hermite approach then a damped, windowed sine tail. Its arrival clamp `limit` is `wobble × scale × ω_d`. It has `value(at:)`, `velocity(at:)` and `retarget(to:arriving:over:at:)`.
- **`LiquidWorld.swift`:**
  - `LiquidSwipe` starts a fresh record when a new swipe opens.
  - `LiquidRun` keeps a prefiltered list of the calls that staged something, for speed.
  - `played(at:)` replays tracks: the latest per channel, linear, hold or 0 after the end, added to the rest.
  - `SeededGenerator` is internal.
- **The S3 rows** are T8 to T17 in `LiquidFlowTests.swift`. Two of them read differently from the plan's text:
  - **T10:** the plan's "centre less than one pitch behind" cannot hold at a change instant, where the swell starts exactly one pitch behind. The row asserts `behind < pitch + wobble`.
  - **T9:** the 0.5 pt jump check compares the history with and without the second call at that instant, not a 1 ms before/after difference.
- **Known open issue in the UI, not yours:** on rows of three apps or fewer, AppKit rebuilds the glass mirror when the band is laid out narrow, so those swipes show the jump look. It is being handled separately. Do not touch LauncherUI.
