# S2: The screen recording hint inside the Switch windows row

## Goal

With Screen Recording missing and *Switch windows* on, the hint shows under that switch inside its row, with the yellow triangle and the grey *Open Settings…* button; the row and the window grow and shrink with it; the first press ever asks macOS, later presses open the Screen Recording page. The notice block below the form holds only the no trackpad notice.

## Requirements

- **R4.** While the screen recording hint applies, it MUST show directly under the *Switch windows* switch, in the same row of the group, its left edge lined up with the row's title. It MUST start with a yellow warning triangle, and its button MUST sit under the message in the ordinary grey style. The hint MUST show while Screen Recording is missing and the *Switch windows* switch is on, and disappear once the permission is granted. The first press of its button, ever, MUST show macOS's own prompt; the fact that it asked survives relaunches. Every later press MUST open the Screen Recording page of System Settings.
- **R5 (the screen recording button).** It reads *Open Settings…*, whether the press asks macOS or opens System Settings. The message stays word for word: "Allow Screen Recording access to show previews in the window switcher."

The window resizes to fit whenever a hint appears or disappears, as it does today.

## Design

Figma values are in `plans/037-settings-window-design/.temp/figma-notes.md` (Switch windows row). `plans/037-settings-window-design/.temp/layout.swift` holds a working probe of this layout (`ScreenRecordingHint` and the `VStack` in `Column`); its line spacing and its 10 pt gap are not the ones to use.

### `SwitcherSection.swift`
- `SwitcherSection` takes `launcher` and `actions: LauncherActions`, like the gesture rows; `SettingsView` passes both.
- Its *Switch windows* entry becomes **one** Form row holding `VStack(alignment: .leading, spacing: 4)`: the existing `SettingRow`, then, while `launcher.screenRecordingHint != nil`, `ScreenRecordingHintView(launcher:actions:)` with a leading inset of `SettingRow.titleInset`, so its leading edge is the title's.
- `SettingRow`'s `HStack` gets an explicit `spacing: Self.titleGap`, a named 8 pt. That is today's measured default, so no row moves (`.temp/gap.swift`: the title sits 53 pt from the row's leading edge). The user decided to keep 8 rather than the frame's 10: a comment may say the frame has 10. `titleInset` is the illustration's width plus `titleGap`. `Illustration.size` is private today: widen its access, do not repeat 45.
- `Switch recent` and `Toggle recent` do not change.

### `ScreenRecordingHintView` (in `SwitcherSection.swift`)
- Takes `launcher` and `actions`, as `GestureRowView` does. The button's action routes on the launcher's hint at press time: `.ask` calls `launcher.askForScreenRecording()`, `.openSettings` calls `actions.openSettings(.screenRecording)`, nil does nothing. The model keeps the first-ever-ask rule and its persistence; nothing in `LauncherCore` changes.
- A leading-aligned VStack, spacing 8. The message is **one `Text`**: the `exclamationmark.triangle.fill` symbol in `#ffcc00`, a space, then `Copy.screenRecordingHint`, in the shared `noticeText()` style, at most 219 pt wide, leading-aligned, wrapping as a paragraph with the symbol inline (`.fixedSize(horizontal: false, vertical: true)`). `Text + Text` is deprecated on macOS 26, so interpolate a coloured `Text` into a `Text`, keeping the message verbatim (`Text(verbatim:)`-safe: it must not be treated as a localisation key that changes it).
- Then `Button(Copy.openSettingsButton, ...)` with `.buttonStyle(.bordered)` and `.buttonBorderShape(.roundedRectangle(radius: 6))`.

### `SettingsView.swift`
- The notice block holds only the no trackpad notice: `if let notice = launcher.inactiveNotice { SettingsNotice(message: notice).padding(.horizontal, Self.inset) }`. Delete `hasNotices`, `noticeSpacing` and the wrapper `VStack`.
- `SettingsNotice` loses its optional button and its custom `init`; delete `NoticeButton`, which only served it. The notice's look (11 pt text, group, 8 pt vertical padding, 42 pt min height) stays. Its doc comment and the `SettingsView` column comment stop naming the tap-bound settings notice and the two permission hints; the column comment now also says the screen recording hint sits in the *Switch windows* row. `labelFont`'s comment stops naming the notices, and any comment on `partSpacing` naming the notices stays accurate.

`SettingsShell`'s fit observation already reads `screenRecordingHint`; change nothing there.
