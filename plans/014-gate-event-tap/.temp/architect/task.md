## What the design must address

1. **The gate's predicate**, as a pure, clock-free value in `LauncherCore`: which recognizer states want the tap (R1 names them: a thumb anchored, a swipe or window swipe in progress, a swap series; the index's C4-C5 show today's `Recognition` has no such output and no warning frame precedes a blocking verdict), which filter memories keep it open (a withheld press through its release, drags and pressure; a dropped or stilled scroll through its momentum; a dropped Dock swipe on either event type), and how the per-trackpad wants and the filters' memory combine. Encode the invariant `blocksClicks ⇒ blocksDockSwipes ⇒ wants` in types rather than prose where you can.
2. **What stops the events.** The probes settle that `tapEnable` is the mechanism (a disabled tap costs what no tap costs; the mask is applied server-side; the call is ~0.1 ms from any thread with synchronous effect). Decide whether presses and releases stay always on in their own tap or the one tap is gated with a predicate wide enough that the frame-side press rule (index C7) never reads a press the filter never saw while a tap gesture or swap could complete. Say which, and show the press-before-landing race, the press right after a grant, and the thumb-and-finger same-frame landing keep today's outcomes.
3. **Who applies `tapEnable` and how the two writers are serialized** (index Q-D): the frame threads change the wants, the tap thread changes the filters' memory; the verdict-drops-while-a-drop-commits race must be one critical section or otherwise structurally closed. Name every shared field's one writer and its readers. State what the frame thread does per frame (a compare, not a call) and the cost of a transition. If the design bends `docs/architecture.md:18`'s one-writer rule or plan 002's "nothing on the frame thread enables or disables the tap", say so and why.
4. **Our own disable's echo and the system's timeout** (index P6, C12, R4): every `tapEnable(false)` we issue delivers a `tapDisabledByUserInput` to the callback; today's handler re-enables unconditionally. Specify the handler: the echo of our own disable versus a disable by the system; what is forgotten; when re-enabling is allowed.
5. **The filters at a gate reopening** (index Q-E, C8-C11): events have passed unseen; the fingers-resting-then-thumb-then-move scroll must still be stilled, not dropped, and a `mayBegin` after reopening must still be a fresh start; say what the Dock filter does and name the hardware check that settles D2. Do not reset memory mid-gesture.
6. **Lifecycle parity** (R4, index C13): `run`/reconcile, Accessibility revoke, hot-plug, wake, hand-mode and setting changes; a fresh tap's initial gate state and how the first-frame cases open it; idempotence of every transition (what happens if it runs twice, or the tap was already in the wished state).
7. **Testability at the behavioural seam** (R2, index C17, Q-G): the handle the in-memory `InMemoryTrackpads` gets so the existing suite runs with the gate modelled (events reach its filters only while the gate is open) and a test can tell "never delivered" from "passed"; the unit seam for the pure gate; what `TapEventTests` pins; what stays manual (the Release-build CPU measurement against 0.088 ms per frame, the hardware checks). The design must give a test a handle on everything it relies on that a test cannot wait for or provoke.
8. **R5**: the sentence in `docs/architecture.md` to replace and what it should say; any other doc sentence the design contradicts.
9. **Scope discipline**: the non-goals stand (no redraw work, no icon memory, no per-frame allocation work beyond what the gate itself needs, no startup work). Prefer the smallest change that delivers R1-R5; no new port unless two adapters exist; no new thread unless a measured need justifies it; no timer, polling or display link; no event posting.

Judge your own interface depth: what the model and `MultitouchTrackpads.run` no longer need to know, what the tap hides, and why the public surface is no larger than needed. Write the usage before the types and reconcile the types to it.
