# S5: A finger-driven wave runs through the beads

## Goal

Finger movement sends a wave through the beads, from the very first movement on. The wave travels in the slide's direction, is biggest near the swell, and grows with finger speed. It is bounded by construction, so the band never breaks and no icon leaves its bead. Like every other motion, it is staged whole at the input event as sampled bead height tracks, and it ends by itself within a second.

## Requirements

- **R8.** Finger movement MUST send a wave through the band in the direction of the slide. The wave is biggest near the swell and weakens with distance from it. Its size MUST grow with finger speed: a fast slide makes a bigger wave than a slow one over the same distance. However fast the slide, the wave MUST NOT break the band, move an icon out of its bead or swell, or raise a bead beyond the wave's own maximum. That maximum is 12 pt beyond the beads on each side by default (as far as the swell rises), and it does not follow the swell's size.
  - *Fast versus slow:* of two swipes that each move the selection by one app, the quick one makes a visibly bigger wave through the band.
  - *Far icons ripple less:* in a row of ten apps with the first selected, the beads near the swell ripple more than the beads at the far end.
  - *Hardest flick:* the band stays one unbroken shape and every icon stays inside its bead or swell.
- **R11 (the opening wave).** Any wave from the opening movement MUST begin at once.
- **R6, R9, R10, R12, whatever the hand.** The swell reaches the newly selected icon within 200 ms. The liquid settles within a second and then stays still. The end pull stays within a fifth of a pitch. Lifting brings the selected app to front at once.
- **R14.** The wave's bounds scale with the row.
- **R15.** The per-event budget: smooth at 120 Hz with ten apps.
- **R16.** While the liquid is settled and while the switcher is hidden, it MUST NOT redraw or run animation work. *Fingers resting on a settled switcher:* two seconds pass, and the switcher draws no new frames.
- **R17 (the motion knobs).** The wave's strength and reach, and the wobble, MUST each be adjustable in one place (`LiquidLook`).

## Design

LauncherCore: `LauncherKit/Sources/LauncherCore/Liquid.swift` (add `WaveTrain` there or in a sibling file). LauncherUI needs nothing new: `SwitcherPanel.apply` already maps `.bead(i)` channels through `GlassMirror.layer(for:)` and `LiquidKeyframes` (key `liquid.bead.<i>.height`, key path `bounds.size.height`).

```swift
/// Crests travelling from the swell in the slide's direction; a bead's offset is the saturated sum of their ripples.
struct WaveTrain: Sendable {
    struct Crest { let emitted: LiquidTime; let origin: Double; let direction: Double; let strength: Double }
    mutating func emit(at position: Double, time: LiquidTime) -> Bool
    func offset(bead: Int, at t: LiquidTime) -> Double   // within the layout's limits
}
```

### When a crest is emitted

- **Under `.flowing` only,** on a call that is a new value (not a repeat), inside the row. Past an end no crest is emitted, so the band and beads stay still during the end pull.
- **On the first move,** the first call after the opening whose value differs. That gives R11's opening wave, even before the selection changes.
- **After that,** whenever `selection.position` has moved at least `waveEvery` (0.5) items since the last crest. A sub-half-step creep makes one crest, at its first move.
- **At most one crest per call.**

### Finger speed (measured inside `Liquid`, on its own clock)

- **Speed** = |position now − position at the last crest| / max(time now − time of the last crest, 0.008 s), in items per second. For the first crest, the reference is the opening call's position and time.
- **Coalescing** only lengthens the span, which still gives the correct average. No new signal crosses the recognizer, the port or the model.
- **Strength** = `waveHeight` × scale × tanh(speed / `waveFullSpeed`). It is bounded however fast the fingers go.
- **Direction** is the sign of the position change.

### A bead's height offset

Bead i's height offset at t is `saturate(Σ over crests of strength × falloff(i) × ripple(t − emitted − d / c))`, where:

- **d** = |i − origin|, in items, with `origin` the position at emission;
- **c** = `waveSpeed` (items per second along the band);
- **falloff** = e^(−d / `waveReach`), times `waveBehind` (0.3) for beads on the side opposite the crest's direction. The wave runs in the slide's direction, biggest near the swell.
- **ripple(τ)** is 0 for τ < 0. Otherwise it is e^(−ζωτ) × sin(ωτ), with ω = 2π × `waveHz` and ζ = `waveDamping`, multiplied by a smooth window that reaches 0 exactly at `settleBound` (1 s) after the crest's emission. So every crest has ended at most 1 s after its call. Distant beads start later and end at the same deadline.
- **saturate** soft-saturates the sum into the layout's limits. Let `rest` = the bead's rest height (`beadSide × scale`), `up` = `beadLimits.upperBound − rest` and `down` = `rest − beadLimits.lowerBound`. For x ≥ 0 the result is `up × tanh(x / up)`; for x < 0 it is `down × tanh(x / down)`. `beadLimits` is already built as (max(`iconBody` + 4, `bandThickness`) × scale) … ((`beadSide` + 2 × min(`waveMax`, `room`)) × scale). So a bead never grows more than `waveMax` beyond its rest on each side, never dips within 4 pt of the icon body, and never goes thinner than the band. This is independent of `swellRise`.
- **What never moves:** the band never animates, the icons never move, and beads carry only height tracks.

### Tracks

- **On a crest,** the frame carries one `.height` track per **real** bead (indices 0 ..< `iconCount`, never a spare). Each is sampled at 120 Hz from the call's time until every crest's ripple has ended, at most `settleBound` after the call, with at least two samples. Its offsets are the saturated sum above.
- **Each new bead track replaces its channel's running one.** It is sampled from the whole crest list, including older crests still ringing, so it starts where the bead is. Bead tracks end at 0 and never hold.
- **The per-event budget (D3).** One `.moved` event stages at most three swell tracks; a crest adds ten bead tracks; a selection change adds one view frame set and the icon grow. Under `.still` there is no crest and no bead track.
- **`shape(at:)`** includes the beads' height offsets: each real bead is its rest rect grown about its centre by the offset. `shape(at:)` and the sampler evaluate the same closed forms, so they agree to 1e-6 pt at every sample instant.

## Starting point

S1 to S4 are committed.

**`Liquid`** (`Sources/LauncherCore/Liquid.swift`):

- It holds the last selection, two `EdgeCurve`s (`left`, `right`), and per-edge flow due times. A finger-follow retarget uses an approach of max(`followTime`, due − time), so a flow is never cut short.
- `EdgeCurve` has a Hermite approach, an optional hold, a second leg back to a rest, a damped, windowed sine tail, an arrival clamp (`wobble × scale × ω_d`), and an optional value bound (the end pull's cap).
- `follow` covers the opening, the repeat, the flow on a change, the lean between changes, the end pull with `pullHold` and spring-back, and the turn back. Edges still inside their flow arrive with the flow's push, clamped to the wobble ceiling.
- `tracks(over:from:)` samples the three swell channels at 120 Hz to the curves' end. The last sample is the curve's real end value, so a held lean ends non-zero and holds.
- `shape(at:)` uses the same closed forms, and today returns the band and beads at rest.
- `beadLimits` exists on `SwitcherRowLayout`, so far unused.
