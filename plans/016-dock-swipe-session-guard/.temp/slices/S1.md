# S1: Dock swipes stay blocked for the whole thumb touch

Repo root (worktree): `/Users/dominykasgrubys/code/trackpad-launcher/.claude/worktrees/016-dock-swipe-session-guard`. Package: `LauncherKit/`. Quick gate (and full gate): `Scripts/test.sh` (runs `swift test` in `LauncherKit`; pass a filter, e.g. `Scripts/test.sh --filter BlockingLevelRecognizerTests`, while iterating). Do not commit; leave the work in the tree.

## Goal

Once a thumb has anchored or landed under resting fingers, the system's desktop, full-screen, Mission Control and App Exposé swipes stay blocked whenever two or more contacts are on the pad, until every contact has lifted, whatever the thumb does meanwhile; and a Dock swipe whose early warning (`mayBegin`) slipped through while the thumb was alone is still dropped from its `began` once the fingers are down. The tap's gated port stays open for as long as either can drop. Doc comments and the test rows that pinned the old rule say the new one.

Background (the bug): macOS counts every contact, so thumb plus two sliding fingers reads as its three-finger swipe. Today Dock swipe blocking follows the recognizer's *anchored thumb*: a thumb that rolls more than 2 mm (`anchorReleaseMarginMM`) past the corner zone becomes a rest finger and the level drops to `.none`; during an unconfirmed window swipe a rolled thumb leaves `.possible`; a confirmed slide whose thumb was lost ends into `.rest`, level `.none`; a thumb that blips and lands back in the release margin lands as a finger. Separately, `DockSwipeFilter` latches `.pass` on a passed `mayBegin`, so the `began` after it passes even if blocking turned on in between.

## Requirements

- **R1. Dock swipes stay blocked until the pad empties after a thumb gesture.** Once a thumb has been in a trackpad's anchor corner (anchored, or landed under resting fingers), or a swipe, window swipe or swap series has started there, that trackpad's blocking level MUST be at least `.dockSwipes` on every frame with two or more contacts down, and at least `.possible` on every frame with one contact down, until a frame where no contact is down. This MUST hold whatever happens to the thumb meanwhile: rolling out of the corner, lifting, or landing again anywhere. Click blocking (`.clicks`) MUST NOT change: it is still decided exactly as today. Contacts the recognizer already ignores (edge contacts) do not count as contacts down.
  - Scenario: thumb anchored, two fingers start a swipe; thumb rolls 30 mm out, fingers lift, land again and slide sideways while the thumb stays → every frame with ≥2 contacts blocks Dock swipes, every frame with one wants the tap, from anchoring until the pad empties.
  - Scenario: thumb rolls out while a window swipe awaits the main actor's answer → those frames block Dock swipes.
  - Scenario: thumb loses contact one frame and comes back 1 mm outside the corner zone, inside the release margin → every frame blocks Dock swipes until the pad empties.
  - Scenario: no thumb anchored since the pad last emptied; three fingers land outside the corner and swipe → no frame blocks, level `.none`, as today.
- **R2. A Dock swipe is judged on its `began`, not on a passed `mayBegin`.** `DockSwipeFilter` MUST judge a Dock swipe again on `began` when its `mayBegin` passed: if blocking is on at `began`, that event and the rest of the swipe MUST be dropped. A swipe whose `mayBegin` was dropped MUST stay dropped whole, a swipe that passed its `began` MUST still pass whole, and a swipe first seen mid-way MUST still pass, as today.
- **R3. The tap's gate keeps its invariant.** The gated port MUST stay open whenever R1 or R2 can drop a Dock swipe, and MUST close once the pad is empty and nothing is withheld, so ordinary trackpad use still routes no high-volume events through the app.
- **R4. Docs match the code.** The doc comments on `Blocking`, `Recognition.blocksDockSwipes` and `DockSwipeFilter` MUST describe the new rules. The tests that assert the old rule MUST be updated to the new one, keeping their click-blocking assertions unchanged. (`docs/architecture.md` and `docs/domain-model.md` are already updated; do not edit them.)

## Design

### Cause 1: the *thumb touch*, inside `GestureRecognizer` (`LauncherKit/Sources/LauncherCore/GestureRecognizer.swift`)

Domain term: a **thumb touch** is the stretch of frames from one with a thumb in the anchor corner (anchored, or landed under resting fingers) until the first frame with no contact on the trackpad, edge contacts aside. Every gesture, swipe, window swipe and swap series lies inside one; what the thumb does inside it changes nothing about its length.

- Fixed behind the existing interface: `step(_:)` keeps returning one `Recognition` with one `Blocking`. Nothing outside the recognizer changes (`DeviceSession`, `mergedBlocking`, `TapContext.estimated(from:)`, in-memory trackpads untouched).
- The recognizer gains one private stored Bool (e.g. `private var isInThumbTouch = false`), kept beside the state machine (`state`), not inside it.
- At the end of `step(_:)`, after today's rules have computed `blocking` (the existing `let blocking: Blocking` if/else chain near the end of `step`, call it today's level):
  - thumb touch is on iff `!down.isEmpty` (the frame is already edge-filtered at the top of `step`, so edge contacts never count) and (it was on before this frame, or today's level `>= .possible`).
  - the returned level is today's level raised to at least `.dockSwipes` while the thumb touch is on with `down.count >= 2`, and to at least `.possible` with `down.count == 1` (i.e. `max(today, floor)`; never lower today's level).
  - Raising never produces `.clicks`, so `blocksClicks` is unchanged by construction.
  - The thumb touch ends on the first frame with no contact down, which today's rules already make `.none`.
- Why the trigger is "today's level ≥ `.possible`": today's rules give `.possible` or more exactly when a thumb is anchored (every state carrying an anchor, or a thumb under a rest), a slide is on (confirmed or not) or a swap series is on, each of which begins with a thumb admitted in the corner.
- The state machine itself is untouched: a rolled thumb is still a rest finger whose next landing is judged anew; swaps, taps and slides are decided as today. Do NOT keep a rolled/lifted thumb as "the thumb", do NOT add a `Blocking` case, do NOT widen the corner or the 2 mm margin.
- `answerWindowSwipe` needs no change (it changes `state` only; the thumb touch Bool is untouched by it).

Doc comments to update in that file (R4):
- `Blocking.none` ("No thumb in the corner, no slide, no swap series: …") gains "and no thumb touch".
- `Blocking.possible`: a thumb anchored alone, a slide awaiting its confirmation, or one contact down in a thumb touch (keep the "gated port must already be receiving" rationale).
- `Blocking.dockSwipes`: a thumb with another contact down (its 3 s over or its landing unknown), or two or more contacts down in a thumb touch.
- `Recognition.blocksDockSwipes`: currently says "true for the whole of a swipe or a window swipe, and while any thumb is anchored and another finger is down, with no time limit" — restate it: true for the whole of a confirmed swipe or window swipe or a swap series, and on every frame of a thumb touch (from a thumb in the anchor corner until the pad is empty) with two or more contacts down, with no time limit; macOS counts every contact, a drifted or re-landed thumb included. The phrase "while any thumb is anchored and another finger is down" must be gone.
- `Swipe.isConfirmed`'s comment ("until the model's answer confirms it, it blocks only by the thumb rules") becomes: it blocks clicks and scrolling only by the thumb rules, and Dock swipes as any thumb touch does. The phrase "blocks only by the thumb rules" must be gone. Also check `answerWindowSwipe`'s comment and others in the file for that phrase.
- Each of `Blocking.none`, `Blocking.possible`, `Blocking.dockSwipes`, `Recognition.blocksDockSwipes`, `Swipe.isConfirmed` comments must contain the lowercase phrase "thumb touch".
- The ordering, `wantsTap`, `blocksDockSwipes`, `blocksClicks` computed properties are unchanged.

### Cause 2: `DockSwipeFilter` (`LauncherKit/Sources/LauncherCore/DockSwipeFilter.swift`)

Each opening event is judged on its own; only a drop is remembered.
- State becomes one stored Bool: a drop under way. `isDropping` (package) is that stored value.
- `decide(_ phase:, trackpadBlocking:)`: while dropping, every phase returns `.drop`, and `.ended` or `.cancelled` end the drop. Otherwise `.mayBegin` and `.began` are each judged on `trackpadBlocking` as they arrive (`.drop` starts a drop, `.pass` remembers nothing), and `.changed`, `.ended`, `.cancelled` pass.
- `forgetSwipe()` clears the drop (keep its doc: the system disabled the tap; forget the swipe in progress, under-blocking).
- Delete `resume()` and its `TODO(D2)` entirely; there is no `.pass` memory any more. The type's doc comment states the new rule: a Dock swipe is kept whole once dropped; each opening event (`mayBegin`, `began`) is judged on its own and only a drop is remembered, so a passed `mayBegin` commits nothing (nothing visible has happened at `mayBegin`) and a `began` under blocking drops the rest of the swipe; a swipe first seen mid-way (or one that passed its `began`) passes, since dropping one already under way could leave the Dock stranded between two desktops.

### `TapFilters.settle` (`LauncherKit/Sources/LauncherCore/TapFilters.swift`)

Remove the line `for stream in dock.keys { dock[stream]?.resume() }`. The predicate (`wantsTap || click.isWithholding || scroll.isSuppressing || any dock isDropping`), the `scroll.resume(...)` call, routing and notice handling are unchanged. The build proves no other caller of `DockSwipeFilter.resume()` remains.

### Gate (R3)

No code change: R1 keeps `wantsTap` true on every frame of a thumb touch with a contact down; R2 starts a drop from `began`, which holds the port open until that swipe ends. Accepted shift: when the thumb lifts while a finger stays down, the port now stays open until the pad empties.
