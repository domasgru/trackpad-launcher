# Testing: Switcher hides apps that have a gesture

## Testing Decisions

### Strategy

One seam, existing: `Launcher` through `World` in `LauncherKit/Tests/LauncherCoreTests/`, the real model, recognizer, click and scroll filters, catalog over a temp tree of fake bundles and settings store over a throwaway suite, with the in-memory hardware, system actions, preferences, access and scanner. It observes `launcher.switcher` (the row's names and locations, `selected`, `selectedApp`, `selectsFrontmost`), `system.broughtToFront`, `hardware.pulses`, `feedback` and `lightTicks`, `system.launchAnimations`, `launcher.isWindowOpen`, `launcher.rows`, `hardware.runCount`, `hardware.pressVerdicts` and `hardware.scroll(_:)`. It misses what LaunchServices really answers for the ⌘-Tab order and the running apps' bundle identifiers (plan 008's adapter tests, unchanged code), the panel's rendering of the row and the icon passes (unchanged code), and the window's controls (R8, below). The new rows resemble `SwitcherOnScreenTests.swift` ("The row and where the selection starts", "Nothing to switch to" and "Trackpad Launcher in front") and `SwapTests.swift`, and live in a new suite beside the former, which is already long. The `Switcher` value's own seam, `SwitcherTests.swift`, gets no new row: every scenario is phrased as a swipe the user starts, and the model seam proves the set reaches the row from the settings; those existing rows pass an empty set and keep their assertions.

T3 and T10 are regression rows: they pass on today's code and guard R1's unassigned case and R7's non-goal, so they are green from the start; once T1 is green, T2, T5, T6, T8 and T9 may pass with no further code, which is the expected shape of a one-rule change, not a sign the rows are redundant.

Faked: nothing we own; the system boundaries are the ones already behind ports (the running-apps read answers what a test set through `makeRunning` and `makeFrontmost`, and LaunchServices' registered copies answer the `Registry`). The clock is the frame timestamps.

Default fixture, unless a row says otherwise: `World(apps:)` with the named apps installed, `start()`, `makeRunning` in the order given (most recently used first) and `makeFrontmost`; assignments through `setAssignment(.app(world.app(name)), for:)`; thumb at (10, 10) mm, right hand, `macBook14`; "begin" is `swipe(3.5, lift: false)`, which starts a swipe and holds it with no travel; "nothing" means nothing brought to front, no pulse, no launch animation and no switcher. The row is read as its apps' names.

### Verified without a test

- **R8, no new control.** The diff touches nothing under `LauncherUI`, `Settings` gains no field, and `SwitcherSection` keeps exactly its two toggles (a read of the file in review), plus a manual step: open the launcher window; the switcher section shows the swipe illustration and switch, the thumb tap illustration and switch, and no other control. No test is the right proof: the window's controls have no model-level representation, and the repo runs no UI tests.
