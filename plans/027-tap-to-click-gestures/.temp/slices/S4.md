# S4: A tap under the anchored thumb never looks up or zooms

Worktree: `/Users/dominykasgrubys/code/trackpad-launcher/.claude/worktrees/027-tap-to-click-gestures` (branch `feature/027-tap-to-click-gestures`). Use absolute paths. Do not commit; the orchestrator commits your work. All source paths below are under `LauncherKit/`.

## Goal

With the Accessibility permission, a three-finger gesture never shows the Look up panel and two quick two-finger gestures never Smart-zoom the page, whatever Tap to click is set to and whether the thumb is still down. A three-finger tap and a two-finger double tap with no thumb still do, and so does Smart zoom without the permission. The click tap learns two new gesture events, the system's *tap actions* Look up and Smart zoom, and drops them under a thumb touch with two or more contacts or in an open lift window (built in S3).

## Requirements

- **R4.** The Look up that macOS shows for a 3-finger tap MUST NOT appear when that tap is under an anchored thumb, or when it arrives up to 0.5 seconds after the tap's fingers lift. A 3-finger tap with no thumb anchored MUST still show Look up. This holds whatever Tap to click is set to.
- **R5.** The Smart zoom that macOS performs for a double tap with two fingers MUST NOT happen when the double tap is under an anchored thumb, or when it arrives up to 0.5 seconds after the second tap's fingers lift. This holds whatever Tap to click is set to. A double tap with two fingers and no thumb anchored MUST still zoom. Blocking needs the Accessibility permission. Without it, the zoom happens as it does today, and Smart zoom never turns gestures off.

## Design

### Hardware facts (measured in S1 on a MacBook trackpad, macOS 27.2)

- Look up arrives as a type-29 (NSEventTypeGesture) event whose field 110 (the IOHID event type) is 24 (the symbolic hot key event), phase field 132 = 5, at the fingers' lift. **It arrives as a PAIR of such events within 0.3 ms**, not one (same field 169; the first carries field 123 = 1 and field 165 = 1, the second does not; both carry fields 115 = 117 = 164 = 70). Each is judged on its own: both fall in the same instant, under the same context, so the stateless rule drops both. No other hid-24 event was seen from other system gestures (Launchpad pinch and Show Desktop spread are type 30 hid 23; an edge swipe is hid 6), so the parser reads every hid-24 type-29 event as Look up, with no sub-field narrowing.
- Smart zoom is ONE type-29 event with field 110 = 22 (the IOHID zoom toggle event), phase 0, at the second tap's lift.
- Returning nothing for these events from the session tap suppresses the panel and the zoom (verified: 56 Look up and 11 Smart zoom events dropped, neither appeared).

### Core

- `TapEvent` (`Sources/LauncherCore/TapEvent.swift`) gains `case tapAction(TapAction)`, with a new `public enum TapAction: Equatable, Sendable { case lookUp, smartZoom }`. `ridesGatedPort` is true for it (type 29 is already in the gated port's mask).
- `TapVerdict` gains `case tapAction(TapActionVerdict)` with `TapActionVerdict` being `pass` or `drop` (or reuse an existing pass/drop verdict type if one fits exactly; a dedicated one is clearer).
- `TapFilters.decide` (`Sources/LauncherCore/TapFilters.swift`): a tap action is dropped when `context.blocking.blocksDockSwipes` (a thumb touch with two or more contacts, which is "under an anchored thumb" however long it rested, and also catches the event when it beats the lift frame to the tap) OR `context.liftWindowIsOpen`; passed otherwise. Each event is decided alone: the rule is stateless and its verdict a plain pass or drop; it changes no filter state. It does NOT depend on `blocksTapClicks` (Look up and Smart zoom are blocked whatever Tap to click is set to). Gate settling is as for every event (`settle` before and after).
- Update `TapEvent`'s doc: every other gesture event (pinch, rotate, an app's swipe) still parses as nothing and passes untouched.

### Platform (`Sources/LauncherPlatform/ClickTap.swift`)

- The parser (`TapEvent.init?(_ type: CGEventType, _ event: CGEvent)`): a type-29 event with field 110 = 24 parses as `.tapAction(.lookUp)`, = 22 as `.tapAction(.smartZoom)`; 23 still parses as a Dock swipe with its phase (types 29 and 30 as today); everything else still nil, so it passes untouched before any lock is taken. Only type 29 carries tap actions. Put the two numbers beside the Dock swipe's in the table of undocumented numbers (`DockSwipeFields`; rename that enum to something covering both, e.g. `GestureEventFields`, if it reads better, updating its doc: "The undocumented event-system numbers the click tap reads gesture events by").
- `TapVerdict.apply(to:)` gains the tap-action case: drop returns nil to the window server; pass returns the event as it came.
- The press and gated masks are unchanged.

### The fake (`Tests/LauncherCoreTests/InMemoryAdapters.swift`)

- `offer(.tapAction(_))` works like any gated-port event: `.noTap` with no tap, `.notSent` while the gated port is off, otherwise decided by the real `TapFilters` at the fake's clock (S3 added it) and returned as `.decided(.tapAction(...))`. Check every `switch` over `TapEvent`/`TapVerdict` in the test support compiles and means something.

## Rules that apply

- architecture.md, Shape: "Framework and wire types never cross a port: `MTTouch`, `CFTypeRef`, `CGEvent` … stay inside adapters and views. External data is parsed into domain types at the adapter." `TapAction` is a domain type; field numbers stay in `ClickTap.swift`.
- architecture.md, Concurrency: "The other [port], over drags, pressure, scrolling and the system's gesture events, is turned on by that thread only while some trackpad is in a thumb touch … or in its lift window (the 0.5 s after fingers lift under the thumb, in which the system's tap actions are blocked) …". No new port, no new mask bit.
- architecture.md, Privacy: "Accessibility … is used only to block … the system's tap actions (the click Tap to click makes, Look up, Smart zoom) under the anchored thumb and in the half second after a gesture …". Nothing outside that scope is dropped.
- architecture.md, Privacy: "the event taps … are each confined to one file by the source-scan test". Keep all `CGEvent` work in `ClickTap.swift`.
- domain-model.md, *Tap action*: "An action macOS performs for a tap of fingers, as opposed to a press: a left click … a right click … the Look up panel for a tap of three (Look up: Tap with three fingers) and Smart zoom for a double tap of two (Smart zoom). … Tap actions are blocked in the lift window; Look up and Smart zoom are blocked also under an anchored thumb with another finger down, however long the thumb has rested. So a gesture never clicks, looks up or zooms; everywhere else tap actions work as macOS makes them. Blocking them needs the Accessibility permission. A tap's click is blocked only while Tap to click is on for a connected trackpad; Look up and Smart zoom whatever the settings."
- Use the glossary words: *tap action*, *Look up*, *Smart zoom*, *lift window*.

## Starting point
