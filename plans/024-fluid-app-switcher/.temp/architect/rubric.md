# Rubric: liquid app switcher design (plan 024)

Success for this task: a maintainer can build the liquid switcher from the sketch. It looks like real Liquid Glass and moves the way R5 to R10 describe, including after the fingers stop. It costs no more than the product's least-resources mandate allows. Its policy is testable without rendering. A future maintainer can retune it, or extend it (for example to the window switcher), without breaking an invariant.

Score each criterion 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent), with one or two sentences of evidence from the candidate.

1. **Requirement coverage and traceability.** Every R1 to R17 and each scenario traces through the usage and the signatures to a named type or function. The time-driven motion (flow and wobble R6, retarget R6/R7, spring-back R5/R10, settle R9, wave decay R8) is explained mechanically, not by assertion. The R8 bounds (no break, icons stay inside, no reach beyond the swell) and the R10 cap are enforced by construction, not by tuning luck.
2. **Fidelity to the resource rule and the platform evidence.** The motion mechanism is chosen against the probed costs (index A4, A5, A7) and the architecture rule (B1, B2), not against assumptions. Its zero-work-at-rest story (R16) is concrete. Any private-API reliance is confined to one place and has a defined fallback. Any rule change is named with its justification. A design that contradicts a probed fact loses here.
3. **Interface depth and locality.** One deep module owns the liquid's geometry and motion policy behind a small interface. Pure policy lives where it can be tested. The AppKit/Core Animation adapter is thin and is the only place that knows the glass mechanism. No information leakage across modules, no pass-through layers, no temporal decomposition. The window switcher and `SwitcherSelection`'s shared behaviour are untouched.
4. **Testability at one named seam.** The seam is named. The motion requirements' scenarios can be driven and observed there with handles for Reduce Motion, finger speed and time, and the absence of the motion mechanism. What remains manual (R2, R15, R16) is stated with how to check it.
5. **Tunability and scaling.** All look and motion numbers live in one value whose defaults are the shipped look, in a module the code that needs them can reach. The swell's size is one setting that makes the swell subtle with nothing else changing (R17). Every dimension scales with the row's scale factor (R14).
6. **Robustness under interruption.** Retargeting composes without queuing. Lift mid-motion, re-open during the close fade, a row of one, ten icons, and the hardest flick all converge to a correct state. The operations are idempotent where they can run twice.
