# S2: Four thumb corners, chosen from the pop-up, with gestures and the palm band in the bottom corners

## Goal

The user opens the settings window, sees a *Thumb corner* row with a pop-up showing the current corner, picks *Bottom left* or *Bottom right*, and from the next thumb every gesture, swipe, window swipe and swap works from that corner with the same click, scroll and Dock swipe blocking and haptics. With a bottom corner, the bottom edge outside the corner is the palm band and the side edges are not. The guide shows only for *Top left*. The choice survives a relaunch, an old record loads as its top corner, and a downgrade to 1.0.6 keeps every other setting. The illustrations still show the top-corner pictures in this slice (S3 adds the bottom ones). The product overview and the code comments are rewritten.

The repository root is `/Users/dominykasgrubys/code/trackpad-launcher/.claude/worktrees/028-thumb-corner-dropdown`; the Swift package is `LauncherKit/`. Work only there.

## Requirements

**R1. The thumb corner is chosen from a pop-up.** The settings window MUST show a *Thumb corner* row in place of the *Left thumb* / *Right thumb* segmented control. Its pop-up offers *Top left*, *Top right*, *Bottom left* and *Bottom right*, in that order, and shows the current choice.
- Scenario: given *Top left*, the user picks *Bottom right*: the pop-up shows *Bottom right*, and the anchor corner is the bottom-right corner for every thumb that lands after the change; a thumb already down when the choice changes is judged afresh, as after any settings change: it anchors only if it rests in the new corner, and otherwise is a plain contact until it lifts.
- Scenario: the user picked *Bottom left* earlier; opening the settings window shows *Bottom left*.

**R2. A bottom corner anchors the thumb at the bottom.** With *Bottom left* or *Bottom right* chosen, the anchor corner MUST be the zone 20% of the width from that side edge and 25% of the height from the bottom edge. A contact anywhere else MUST NOT anchor. An anchored thumb MUST stay anchored until it moves more than 2 mm outside the zone.

**R3. Every gesture works the same from a bottom corner.** The tap gestures, the swipe, the window swipe, the swap (a thumb tap under two resting fingers), click blocking, scroll suppression, Dock swipe blocking and the haptic feedback MUST behave in a bottom corner exactly as they do in a top corner. Slide directions MUST stay the same: sliding right reaches older apps, and up and down drive the window swipe as today.

**R4. Bottom corners have their own palm band.** With a bottom corner chosen, an edge contact MUST be a contact landing closer than 3 mm to the bottom edge, outside the anchor corner. The side edges MUST NOT be part of the band, and a contact landing inside the anchor corner MUST NOT be an edge contact. An edge contact is ignored for as long as it stays down: it neither starts nor joins a tap, a swipe or a swap.

**R5. Top corners keep today's palm band.** With a top corner chosen, a contact landing closer than 3 mm to the bottom edge anywhere along it, or closer than 3 mm to the edge opposite the thumb and more than 40% of the height down from the top, MUST be an edge contact.

**R7. The guide shows only for the top-left corner.** The *Hold* / *Tap* guide over the first illustration MUST show only while the thumb corner is *Top left*.

**R8. Saved choices migrate.** An existing saved *Left thumb* MUST load as *Top left*, and *Right thumb* as *Top right*. (Upgrading with *Right thumb*: at login the thumb anchors top-right with no window opened; opening the window shows *Top right*.)

**R9. A first run defaults to the top-left corner.**

**R10. The choice survives a relaunch.**

**R11. A downgrade keeps the other settings.** After a downgrade to a version without bottom corners, every other setting MUST survive: app assignments and every switch. A saved bottom corner MUST load there as the top corner on the same side.

## Design

### `ThumbCorner` (`LauncherCore/ThumbCorner.swift`, from S1)

Add `bottomLeft` and `bottomRight`, after `topLeft`, `topRight`, so `allCases == [.topLeft, .topRight, .bottomLeft, .bottomRight]`. Still `Hashable`, `Sendable`, `CaseIterable`, **not** `Codable`, no raw value. Its package-internal geometry queries are exactly the three the recognizer needs:
- whether the corner is a bottom one (the palm band's key);
- how far a normalised x lies from the corner's side edge, as a fraction of the width (left corners: `x`; right corners: `1 - x`);
- how far a normalised y lies from the corner's top or bottom edge, as a fraction of the height (top corners: `y`; bottom corners: `1 - y`). Remember `SurfacePoint.y` is 0 at the TOP edge and 1 at the bottom.

### The saved record (`LauncherCore/Settings.swift`)

The decoding rule is unchanged (missing key = default, unknown key ignored, undecodable value fails the whole record). A four-value enum under the existing `handMode` key would break a downgrade: 1.0.6 fails the whole record on a raw value it does not know and loads every assignment as unassigned. So the corner is stored as **two private fields**, with `public var thumbCorner` computed over them (get and set):
- the side under the key `handMode` with the values `right` and `left` exactly as every version has written them (the hand: `right` = thumb on the left side), as S1 left it;
- `isThumbAtBottom: Bool`, under the new key `isThumbAtBottom`, default `false`, read in `init(from:)` with `decodeIfPresent` like every other field.

The synthesized encoding is kept (so a record now always writes both keys). This gives R8–R11 with no migration code: an old record has only the side, so *Left thumb* loads as *Top left* and *Right thumb* as *Top right*; no record is the defaults, *Top left*; 1.0.6 reads the side, ignores the new key and anchors in the top corner on that side.

Mapping: `topLeft` = (`right`, false), `topRight` = (`left`, false), `bottomLeft` = (`right`, true), `bottomRight` = (`left`, true).

### The anchor corner (`LauncherCore/Surface.swift`, `AnchorCorner`)

The same zone measured from the chosen corner's two edges. `admits(p)`: fraction from the side edge `< GestureRules.cornerWidth` (0.20) **and** fraction from the top/bottom edge `< GestureRules.cornerHeight` (0.25). `holds(p)`: the same zone grown by `anchorReleaseMarginMM` (2 mm) on both axes, compared in millimetres as today. In `GestureRules.swift`, `cornerHeight`'s comment changes from "measured from the top edge" to "measured from the corner's top or bottom edge"; no number changes. Slide travel is already independent of the corner (positive to the right and positive downwards by construction), so R3's directions need no code change; tests prove it.

### The palm band (`LauncherCore/Surface.swift`, `EdgeContacts.isInBand`)

One rule keyed by the corner's row:
- **Top corner**: today's band, unchanged: closer than 3 mm (`edgeBandMM`) to the bottom edge anywhere along it, or closer than 3 mm to the side edge opposite the thumb corner and more than 40% (`edgeBandTop`) of the height down.
- **Bottom corner**: closer than 3 mm to the bottom edge **and** at least 20% of the width (`cornerWidth`) from the corner's side edge, so the strip stops exactly where the anchor corner's width begins; no side edge is in the band. A landing inside the anchor corner is never an edge contact, so a thumb hugging the bottom edge in its corner anchors.

The band is decided at landing, as today; the edge-contact bookkeeping (which contacts are palms while down) is untouched. Update `EdgeContacts`' doc comment and `edgeBandTop`'s / `edgeBandMM`'s comments in `GestureRules.swift` to say the side-edge part applies to top corners.

### The model

No new model code beyond S1: `thumbCorner` / `setThumbCorner(_:)` already exist; a corner change re-runs the trackpads with fresh recognizers exactly as before, ending any swipe in progress; a thumb already down is judged on the fresh recognizer's first frame (domain model *Thumb touch*: "Recognition restarting mid-touch … starts a thumb touch only if a thumb is in the corner on its first frame"; *Blocking window*: a thumb already resting opens no window but anchors).

## Rules that apply

- `docs/architecture.md`, *Shape*: "`LauncherCore` (domain types, pure policy, ports, the models; imports Foundation and Observation only)". `ThumbCorner`, `Copy`, the geometry stay Foundation-only.
- `docs/architecture.md`, *Shape*: "Framework and wire types never cross a port … `UserDefaults` keys … stay inside adapters and views." The two wire fields are private to `Settings`.
- `docs/architecture.md`, *Shape*: "Policy lives in pure functions and value types; the shell stays thin." The guide rule and title strings are values; the view only wires them.
- `docs/architecture.md`, *Concurrency*: "Multitouch frames are recognised on the framework's own thread … State shared with that thread lives behind a `Mutex` with one writer." Do not add any path that retargets a running recognizer; a corner change re-runs through the reconcile, as every settings change does.
- `docs/architecture.md`, *Concurrency*: "No timers, no polling, no background loops."
- `Settings.swift`, the decoding rule: "a key missing from the record decodes as that field's default; a key this build does not know is ignored; a key whose value does not decode fails the whole record." Each new field's doc comment says "The key is this field's name, permanently." like the others.
- `docs/domain-model.md`, *Edge contact*: "With a top thumb corner: closer than 3 mm to the bottom edge, anywhere along its length, or closer than 3 mm to the *hand-side edge*, the side edge opposite the thumb corner, and more than 40% of the trackpad's height down from the top. With a bottom thumb corner: closer than 3 mm to the bottom edge and outside the anchor corner; no side edge is in the band … a contact landing inside the anchor corner is a thumb however close to the bottom edge. … An edge contact is ignored for as long as it stays down: it neither starts nor joins a tap, a swipe or a swap, and it is not another contact for arming, click blocking or Dock swipe blocking."
- `docs/domain-model.md`, *Swap*: "exactly two fingers rest outside the anchor corner (edge contacts aside)".
